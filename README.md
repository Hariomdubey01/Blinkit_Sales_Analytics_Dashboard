# 📖 Overview

Data becomes valuable only when it can answer business questions.

This project showcases an **interactive Blinkit Sales Analytics Dashboard** developed in **Microsoft Power BI** using a retail sales dataset. The dashboard transforms raw sales transactions into meaningful insights that help evaluate outlet performance, identify top-performing product categories, monitor customer ratings, and support data-driven decision-making.

Designed with a clean, modern interface, the dashboard enables users to explore business performance through interactive filtering, dynamic visualizations, and executive-level KPIs—all within a single report.

---

# 🎯 Project Objectives

The dashboard was designed to help answer key business questions, including:

- Which outlet sizes and locations generate the highest sales?
- Which product categories and fat-content types contribute the most to overall revenue?
- How does outlet establishment year affect revenue consistency?
- What is the overall sales volume and average transaction performance?
- How are customers rating products, and what does that signal about quality?
- Which outlet tiers (Tier 1/2/3) perform best?

# 📊 Key Business Insights

The dashboard reveals several meaningful business patterns:

- Medium-sized outlets contributed the highest share of total sales (**$507.90K**), making them the best-performing outlet format.
- Fruits & Vegetables and Snack Foods generated the highest revenue, indicating strong customer demand in these categories.
- Tier-3 outlets accounted for the highest share of total sales (**$207.37K**), outperforming Tier-1 and Tier-2 locations.
- Regular Fat products contributed a notably higher share of total sales than Low Fat alternatives.
- Older outlets show more consistent revenue performance than newly established ones.

# 🧹 Data Preparation

Before building the dashboard, the dataset was prepared using **Power Query** to ensure consistency and accuracy.

The data preparation process included:

- Importing raw sales data
- Removing duplicate records
- Handling missing values
- Validating and correcting data types
- Standardizing inconsistent category labels (e.g., merging "low fat" / "LF" / "Low Fat")
- Structuring the dataset for reporting and analysis

**Dataset:** Blinkit Sales Dataset

| Attribute | Detail |

- Item Identifier & Item Type

- Item Fat Content
- Item MRP & Sales
- Outlet Identifier
- Outlet Establishment Year
